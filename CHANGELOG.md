# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes, newest first. Categories: **Added** (new
features), **Fixed** (bug/behaviour fixes), **Removed** (things taken out),
**Info** (notes worth knowing).

## [0.7.0] – 2026-10-09

### Added
- **Navigation**: a new Navigation tab (between Map and Power Curve) routes you from your car to a destination you click on a map. Choose which road types to use (Road, Highway, Dirt, Trail, Cross-country, Jumps; Road, Highway and Dirt are on by default) and a Faster roads / More curves slider; the tab shows the remaining distance and time (in miles when the app is set to mph). Click the tab's map to set the destination; in the Map tab's viewer use the **Set destination** button or Shift+click (a plain click there is still the co-op waypoint, a right click still clears it). Navigation keeps a live route from the car, re-routes when you leave it, pauses in races and follows a co-op teammate's destination. The navigation route is drawn on the HUD minimap, the Dashboard map and the Map-tab viewer (flat, tilted and 3D) as a fuchsia road with a destination pin; hidden during races. It clears itself when you arrive and your destination is remembered across restarts. In a co-op session your destination is shared with the room (also one you had set before joining, unless the room already has one): teammates route to it from their own car with your road types, and anyone can clear it for everyone (switch sharing and following off in the tab's Co-Op card). The route's on/off, colour and width are per map on the Map tab (Navigation route card). The game's files do not say which roads are one-way, so on a divided highway the route may use the wrong side.
- **3D map: teammates as 3D cars** — co-op teammates are shown with the same 3D car or arrow as your own car (whichever you picked as Car marker), in their own colour, at their real position and height, so a teammate in a tunnel is down in the tunnel.
- **3D map view**: the HUD minimap, the Dashboard map and the Map-tab viewer can show the island in 3D (Map tab → Minimap / Dashboard map / Viewer → View mode → 3D): terrain relief from your game install with the satellite image draped on it and the roads as ribbons with decks at their real heights, with your arrow, trail, teammates, race lines and points of interest. The HUD minimap keeps its rounded pill. Drag and scroll move and zoom the map with your pointer, and a click for a shared waypoint lands on the hill you point at. While the terrain loads, or if your graphics card can't do it (too old, or too slow), the map draws Tilted by itself and the View mode card says why. Tilted stays the default; 3D is opt-in per map. Experimental on Windows: there it is "3D (experimental)" and needs **Allow 3D on Windows** on the View mode card first.
- **Minimap frame options** (Overlay tab → Minimap): size (width and height), outline (width, colour, opacity), background (colour, opacity), the corner radius, and a round minimap (a circle with one diameter setting). The map, its markers and the 3D view are cut exactly to the chosen shape, and the compass moves inside it; the minimap's footprint in the layout follows its size. The defaults are the old look, so nothing changes until you touch them. "Map layers…" jumps to the Map tab for what the map shows.
- **3D car and trail**: in the 3D map view your car and its trail are drawn in 3D at the car's real position and height. In a tunnel the car stays at the tunnel road instead of floating over the hill, and the trail shows through the hill. Pick a 3D arrow (default) or a small car model in the View mode card.
- **Race routes drawn as a road**: a race is now drawn as a road of its own in the race colour (outline, rounded ends, as wide as a highway) instead of a thin line on top. In 3D it is a real road at the race's own heights: it stays on bridges, shows through hills in tunnels and passes under overpasses, and it works for cross-country races too. Map tab → Race lines → **Race line style** switches back to the old thin line.
- **Race road only**: in a race, "Other roads" (Map tab → Race lines → In a race) can now be set to **Race road only**, which shows nothing of the road network, only the race road.
- **First-run setup guide**: on the first launch a step-by-step guide leads you through the setup: find the game install, check the input permissions (Linux), test window detection, then enter the Data Out values in Forza (with a screenshot, your listen port and a live "packets arriving?" check), and finally a short tour of the Mini-Settings and the hotkeys. You can skip it and re-open it any time from Setup → Getting started. Existing installs do not see it on update.

### Fixed
- **Co-op: joining while the game is not running**: a player who joined a Cloudflare-hosted session before starting Forza was not added to the room and kept reconnecting every 10 seconds until telemetry flowed; joining now completes at once.
- **3D map: race lines stay behind hills** — race lines in every mode, the thin line style and the start / finish marks are now hidden behind hills and overpasses like the roads, instead of showing through them.
- **3D map: hills stay put** — hills and mountain ridges no longer pop or shift while driving; the terrain detail levels now blend smoothly into each other.
- **Jump lines on the HUD minimap** are cut to the minimap's shape like the roads, instead of poking out of a rounded corner.
- **Settings no longer reset completely** when one saved value can't be read (e.g. after switching versions): only that setting goes back to its default (a single unreadable dashboard widget only drops itself), and the old file is kept as `config.json.bad-…`. If `config.json` is unusable as a whole, your settings are restored from your active profile instead of defaults, and that profile's file is backed up too before anything overwrites it. The same goes for profiles and the saved gearbox calibrations (kept as `….bad-…` next to the file); files with broken text encoding are salvaged instead of replaced.
- **Roads can grow wider when zoomed far in**: the road width limit (Map tab → Roads → Maximum width, and the minimum and fixed widths) now goes up to 100 px instead of 30 px, so with the width-follows-zoom rule roads keep growing as you zoom in instead of stopping at the limit. Set the maximum as high as you like to get the behaviour you want; the defaults are unchanged.
- **3D map: dragging and zooming follow the pointer**: dragging and the mouse wheel now move and zoom the map with the pointer, anchored on the terrain under it, with no more jumps or stalls on hills. Waypoint clicks on slopes land exactly, and the Dashboard map's Follow car button no longer overlaps the compass.
- **No more stutter every 2 seconds**: the window no longer stutters for about 150 ms every 2 seconds on systems with many input devices, and the packets-per-second readout in the status bar now stays steady (it used to swing between about 60 and 80 at a constant 70 packets per second). Acceleration and braking test times are also no longer affected by UI hiccups.
- **Race detection waits until it is sure**: the current race is no longer picked at once. It keeps every route that fits where you have driven, shows only the part they share (no finish line yet) up to the fork, and draws the full route with its finish once it is certain (typically about 25 m after the fork). In a multi-lap circuit race the map no longer switches to another route sharing the start line when a new lap begins; once identified, the race stays for the rest of the race.
- **Cleaner map in a race**: with other roads muted or hidden, side roads joining the route no longer leave a short stub, and highways passing above or below the route no longer show as floating pieces (2D and 3D).
- **Roads join cleanly**: a road continuing through a type change or around a corner is one mitred line, dead ends and junctions are rounded, and outlines no longer cut across a junction. In 2D, roads crossing over each other (overpasses, interchange ramps) keep their outline. HUD minimap, Dashboard map and Viewer, 2D and 3D.

### Info
- **One race colour.** Sprints and circuits are now drawn in the same colour, set with the single **Race colour** setting (Map tab → Race lines), orange by default instead of the same blue as normal roads. A saved circuit colour at the old default blue is switched to orange automatically, and a colour you chose yourself is kept (the separate sprint colour is gone).
- **The Map tab viewer now uses the Dashboard map's settings**: the viewer and the Dashboard map are drawn with one set of settings (layers, north-up, mirror at edges, compass, allow pan and zoom, zoom radius), so there is a single page for both, **Dashboard map & Viewer**, and "Copy to…" only has the Minimap and that page left. The viewer's own settings from earlier builds are no longer used (your other settings are kept). The viewer's Settings button moved to the bottom right, so it no longer sits on the co-op player list.
- **Map settings only on the Map tab**: Mini-Settings (the cog on the status bar) and the Co-Op tab no longer have any map settings, not even the Dashboard's "Map" module switch (now **Show Dashboard map** on the Map tab → Dashboard map & Viewer). The Dashboard → Map page and the Map and Co-Op sections of the Overlay page are gone; everything is on the Map tab, where most of it already was. Moved there: Render FPS limit and the co-op trail fade and player list (Dashboard map & Viewer page), the co-op options of the HUD minimap (Minimap page), and the image quality, Reload Map, Rebuild Map Cache and calibration (Map data page, new Map image card).
- **Viewer: Follow car only when needed**: the Map tab viewer's Follow car button now only shows while the view is not following your car (after you dragged or zoomed), like on the Dashboard map.

## [0.6.0] – 2026-10-08

### Added
- **Roads, points of interest and race lines on the Dashboard map**: the map now draws the game's roads over the satellite image, each type in its own colour (road blue, highway yellow, off-road orange, trails dashed, tunnels and jump lines too), plus your race: while you are in a race, that race's racing line is drawn (best effort, the game doesn't tell the app which race it is). Zoom in below a 3 km radius to see the points of interest (barn finds, car meets, fast travel, houses, speed traps and zones, XP boards and more) as coloured markers. Everything comes from your own Forza Horizon 6 install and updates within a second when you save or reset road types in the map editor. Without the game installed the map stays as it was. The settings are on the Map tab (Dashboard map).
- **The in-game HUD minimap shows the same roads, points of interest and race lines, tilted by default**: the minimap now draws the Dashboard map's layers over a dimmed satellite image (50 % opacity, brightness and saturation, no plate behind it, so the game shows through), seen at an angle with the far end fading out and distant roads thinning, 300 m around you, turning with your heading. Roads, race lines and markers are cut to the pill's rounded corners, teammates, trails and waypoints follow the tilt, and the map keeps updating while the game covers the app window. The race line shows only while you are in a race. The settings are on the Map tab (Minimap); fresh installs get these defaults, your saved overlay settings are not changed.
- **Points of interest use the game's own icons** on both maps (read from your install, never shipped with the app): speed traps, zones and trailblazers with a line across the road where their gate is, danger signs, barn finds, car meets, houses, fast travel boards and so on, with the current treasure chest drawn bigger. Which chest is "current" is inferred from the weekly Thursday 14:30 UTC rotation and not verified yet. A kind the game has no icon for stays a coloured marker.

- **Overlay tab: module tabs**: a selector at the top of the Overlay tab (General, Drive cluster, Race / Drift, Notifications) shows one module's settings at a time, and it remembers where you were.
- **Map tab**: a new tab (map icon, right after Overlay) with the whole map full size. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom (around the cursor), "Follow car" puts the view back on you, and the cog switches the tab to a full-size settings view with the pages Minimap, Dashboard map, Viewer and Map data (the map editor). The viewer has its own layer settings (roads, points of interest at every zoom level, race lines, image look, tilt) and shows your car, trail and co-op teammates. Every layer setting of a map is laid out the same: satellite image look, roads (width rule and, per road type, visibility, colour, width, dash, opacity and outline), points of interest (icon size, the zoom radius they show at, gate lines and every category with the game's icon next to it), race lines (which ones, width, colours), the tilted view, plus zoom and orientation. The minimap tab greys out its layer settings while "Use Dashboard map settings" is on and shows the Dashboard's values instead. Mini-Settings keeps its quick options.
- **Pan and zoom the Dashboard map and the Map tab viewer**: drag the map to move it and scroll to zoom, then it goes back to following your car, at the configured zoom, as soon as you start driving again (while you are stopped, or no telemetry arrives, your view stays; a short nudge doesn't snap it back). A "Follow car" button brings it back at once. Both can be switched off with **Allow pan and zoom** (Dashboard map: Mini-Settings or the Map tab; viewer: Map tab). Not in the dashboard's layout-edit mode, where dragging moves the module.
- **"Copy to…" on every map settings card**: at the end of each card on the Map tab's Minimap, Dashboard map and Viewer pages (image, tilted view, race lines, roads, points of interest, and the view options) a small **Copy to…** menu overwrites that one card on the Minimap, the Dashboard map, the Viewer or both others with what you see, so a colour you set once doesn't have to be set again on the other maps. The button shows "Copied" for a moment. While the Minimap follows the Dashboard map it can't be copied into (greyed, with the reason); copying from it uses the Dashboard map's values. The view options between the viewer and the other maps cover lock north-up, mirror at edges, compass and Allow pan and zoom (the viewer has no separate driving / stopped zoom).
- **In a race, the other roads fade out**: while a race line is detected ("Current race" mode, you are in a race) the roads away from the race are shown muted (faint white by default) or hidden, and the points of interest are hidden, so the race stands out. Race lines card, section **In a race**: other roads Normal / Muted / Hidden, the muted colour, opacity and width, and whether points of interest are hidden. Outside a race, or when no race line was detected, nothing changes.

### Fixed
- **Tilted map fills its whole area**: in tilted view the HUD minimap and Dashboard map now fill the map area edge to edge; the far fade only appears near the horizon at steep tilts, and far-away POI icons no longer pile up at the top.

### Info
- **Map settings moved**: the Minimap and Dashboard map settings are now on the Map tab (they were on the Overlay tab) and the map editor / Map data card too (it was in Setup). Nothing was lost; the Overlay tab keeps the HUD's own settings, Setup is shorter.
- **New Dashboard map defaults on a fresh install**: north-up, 1.5 km radius driving / 4.5 km stopped and no compass. Your saved settings are not changed.
- **New default map look** (fresh installs): roads by type, zoom, points of interest (a shorter default list, visible up to a 10 km view; on the minimap only nearby ones, 500 m radius, 40 deg tilt, satellite at full opacity). Existing settings are kept.
- **New HUD minimap default on a fresh install**: a 300 m radius while driving (was 500 m). Your saved overlay settings are not changed.

## [0.4.2] – 2026-10-04

### Fixed
- **Settings now save by themselves (Window Detection title, ports, hotkeys, ...)**: most Setup fields, such as the Window Detection *game window title*, the Detect button, ports and sliders, were only written to disk when you closed the Mini-Settings or quit the app cleanly, so the value reset to the old one if the app was closed any other way (killed by a launcher, a compositor close, a crash, SIGTERM). The app now checks once a second and writes any changed setting within about a second, and still saves on a clean exit.
- **Fresh install picks the window-detection method for your desktop (Linux)**: a new config always used *Hyprland* (`hyprctl`), so on GNOME the game-window check failed and the overlay's *Only when game window is focused* never worked until you changed Setup → Window Detection by hand. It now starts as *Hyprland* on Hyprland, *GNOME (Window Calls extension)* on GNOME and *X11* on other X11 sessions (unknown Wayland compositors keep *Hyprland*). Existing saved settings are not changed.
- **Input permissions: the lights now show whether input really works, and recover by themselves (Linux)**: *Key input* was green when `/dev/uinput` merely opened, even if the virtual keyboard could not be created and every Gearbox / Backfire key press was silently dropped, and after you fixed the cause it stayed dead until a restart. It is now green only when the virtual keyboard was created, and the app keeps retrying every 2 s (**Re-check** retries at once). *Hotkeys* keeps its real-keyboard check, which is now **live**: the status refreshes every couple of seconds on every tab (not only while Setup is open), keyboards you plug in, reconnect over Bluetooth or get access to are picked up within a few seconds without clicking Re-check, and a keyboard's "counts as working" state is no longer frozen when it was first opened. If input turns from working to missing during a session, the "input permissions missing" dialog shows once more (not repeatedly); it closes by itself once everything works.
- **Hotkeys on GNOME/Fedora: virtual devices and mouse interfaces no longer count as a working keyboard**: the permission check still went amber ("fine") when something other than your real keyboard was readable, such as ydotool's or a remapper's virtual keyboard or a gaming mouse's key interface, so the "input permissions missing" dialog stayed away while no key press could be read. Only real keyboards count now (virtual devices and mouse/pad interfaces are still read, but ignored for the status), so you get the red status, the dialog and the fix commands. The check also refreshes every couple of seconds while Setup is open, and a key reader that stops (device unplugged, read error) is logged instead of dying silently.
- **Hotkeys on GNOME/Fedora: the permission check no longer shows a false green**: *Setup → Input Permissions* counted any readable input device, so a game controller made it green even when the keyboard could not be read, and the "input permissions missing" dialog never appeared while hotkeys stayed dead. It now checks that a **keyboard** is readable (and that hotkeys actually opened one), shows red with the fix commands otherwise, and **Re-check** reopens keyboards without a restart. Not being a member of the `input` group is now **red** on its own and opens the "input permissions missing" dialog with `sudo usermod -aG input $USER` (there is no amber state any more): without the group a keyboard practically can't be read, and a gaming mouse's key interface can make the keyboard check look fine. If the dialog is a false alarm for your setup, **Don't remind me again** silences it for good.
- **Automatic gearbox: the detected max RPM (redline) is now locked once calibration finishes**: later over-revs (limiter bounce, downshift spikes) kept raising it and moved the shift point. Only **Clear RPM calibration**, switching cars, or loading a saved per-car calibration changes it now. The Debug tab's Calibration checks show the lock ("Redline not locked" row, heading "locked").

## [0.4.1] – 2026-10-02

### Fixed
- **Max RPM now detected on cars with fast rev limiters**: the capture required positive engine power, but such cars report zero power while bouncing off the limiter, so their max RPM was never captured. The check is gone (it only guarded against mis-shifts, which the current calibration protocol makes practically impossible).
- **Forced-induction detection on the Dashboard**: the Power Graph module's boost line ignored *Forced induction detection* and was drawn for every car, so naturally aspirated cars got a flat boost line at 0 plus a boost scale. It now follows the detection like the Power Curve tab and the Boost Graph module do. Detection is also stricter: a car only counts as turbo/supercharged once boost goes above 1 PSI (was 0.05 PSI, too close to an NA engine's near-zero reading at full throttle), it judges the data actually shown rather than a saved reference from another car, and it starts over when you switch cars.
- **Right-stick look-around no longer jolts**: holding the right stick while *North up when stopped* kicked in (or the map snapped back to heading-up as you drove off) made the map jerk toward north and then swing back to where the stick pointed. Now, while you hold the stick, the map stays exactly where the stick points relative to your car, whatever the map's own rotation does underneath; when you let go it eases smoothly (the short way round) to north-up if you are stopped with that option on, or to heading-up. Dashboard map and HUD minimap alike.
- **Right-stick map rotation in north-up**: with the map locked north-up, looking around with the right stick turned the map relative to north (stick right always put east at the top), so the view seemed randomly offset depending on where the car was heading. The stick now always looks relative to your car, like the game camera: right shows what is to the car's right, down looks behind it, in north-up and heading-up alike, on the Dashboard map and the HUD minimap. Releasing the stick still eases back to north.
- **Rebinding no longer triggers the action**: while you are binding a key or controller button (Setup → Hotkey / Controller, Overlay → Hide HUD), no hotkey or controller action fires, and the key or button you just pressed (or its release) stays silent for a moment afterwards. Before, rebinding Backfire to B while it was already on B toggled Backfire.
- **Setup layout**: the Controller card's bind buttons now all line up at the same position and size in every row (before, each row sat a few pixels further right than the one above), the x clear buttons are gone (press Backspace or Delete while binding instead), and the Network card moved above Game Install in the right column.
- **Controller stick deadzone** now defaults to 0.27 (was 0.15), which ignores the drift of worn sticks. Existing settings keep their saved value.
- **HUD Pill gear position**: the gear indicator in the Pill style of the Drive cluster sat 1 px too low and now sits 1 px higher.

### Added
- **In-game HUD overlay on Windows (experimental, untested)**: the HUD can now be drawn over a borderless/windowed Forza on Windows too, not just on Linux. It is a click-through, always-on-top, non-activating transparent window on one monitor, using the same drawing, settings, visibility rules (pause, Window Detection focus, Hide HUD hotkey) and notifications as the Linux overlay. Exclusive fullscreen can't be overlaid, so set the game to borderless. The Overlay tab is enabled on Windows, with a tooltip noting that only Borderless or Windowed works. Monitor Detection defaults to *Active window* there (the monitor the focused game window is on; Hyprland and Custom command are Linux-only). The Fixed monitor field takes `DISPLAY2`, `\\.\DISPLAY2` or just `2` (default: the primary monitor). Written without access to a Windows machine (only smoke-tested under Wine), so expect rough edges; `FORZA_OVERLAY_TEST=1` shows a test pattern without the game.
- **Debug: Calibration checks**: the Debug tab's "Derived from telemetry" card now lists every condition behind RPM calibration, each with a green or red dot, its live value and its threshold: Max RPM capture (race on, power, handbrake, tyre slip per wheel), Calibrated (the first manual upshift that engages the gearbox) and Gear-map sample (speed, RPM vs redline, tyre slip and suspension per wheel, driving straight), plus the max RPM captured so far and the samples and redline speed per gear. If calibration will not work for a car, you can now see which check is failing.
- **Solo trail (white)**: your own trail now also shows on the Dashboard map and the HUD minimap when you are not in a co-op session, in white like your arrow, with the same fade settings (Mini-Settings → Dashboard → Map → Co-Op "Tracer fade"; the HUD's Co-Op trail fade). In a session it turns your co-op colour as before; the trail you already drove is kept when a session starts or ends. On the HUD, **Show trails** switches it off together with the co-op trails.
- **Delete clears a hotkey too**: while rebinding a keyboard hotkey (Setup → Hotkey, Overlay → Hide HUD), **Delete** now clears it to *Not set* just like **Backspace**; Esc still cancels.
- **HUD minimap = Dashboard map**: the in-game minimap now draws like the Dashboard map. Your own arrow uses the Dashboard's arrow in your co-op colour (Co-Op → Your Identity; white outside a session) with your trail behind it, teammates get the same arrows, names, edge pointers, paused-grey and fading trails, and shared waypoints show up too. New options in Mini-Settings → Overlay: **Mirror map at edges**, a Co-Op section (teammates, waypoints, trails, trail fade), and two tick boxes, **Use Dashboard map settings** and **Use Dashboard co-op settings**, that make the HUD use the Dashboard's values for the map view or for co-op (separately) and hide the HUD's own controls for that part.
- **Look around with the right stick**: new option *Rotate with right stick* for the Dashboard map (Mini-Settings → Dashboard → Map) and the HUD minimap (Mini-Settings → Overlay → Minimap), both on by default. While you push the controller's right stick past its deadzone the map turns to look where the stick points relative to your car (up = ahead, right = the car's right, down = looking back); on release it eases back. Works in heading-up and north-up, the compass stays correct, and it works independently of any right-stick button bindings.
- **Controller input**: a new **Controller** card in Setup (below Hotkey). Bind controller buttons to the in-game actions (Toggle Automatic Gearbox, Clear RPM calibration, Clear gear map, Toggle Backfire, Hide HUD): click a row, press the button on your pad (Esc cancels; Backspace or Delete clears the binding). Works with the A/B/X/Y, bumpers, triggers, Back/Start, stick clicks, D-pad and the right stick's four directions. Stick and trigger deadzones are adjustable. Xbox-style pads on Linux (xpad, xone incl. the wireless dongle, xpadneo) and Windows (XInput); pads plugged in later are picked up automatically, and the real pad is read rather than Steam's virtual one so it keeps working while the game is focused. Linux needs the same `input` group access as the hotkeys.
- **Setup: Game Install**: a new card where you set the Forza Horizon 6 install folder (used for car names). *Auto-detect* finds it through Steam (all Steam libraries; on Windows also via the registry), *Detect from running game* reads it from the running game (Linux/Proton and Windows), or type the path yourself. A status light shows whether the folder was found and how many cars it holds.
- **HUD notifications**: short messages on the in-game HUD when something changes: *Gearbox: ON / OFF*, *Gearbox mode: Race* (also on the automatic switch to Race in a race and back), *Backfire: ON / OFF*, *Calibration started* and *Calibration done: N rpm*, with a *Shift at redline* hint (yellow dot) in between as soon as gear 1 has collected (new) gear-map data while the gearbox is still uncalibrated (once per calibration; it tells you to rev out and shift). *Calibration started* shows the moment you Clear RPM calibration or Clear gear map (hotkey, controller or button; also while paused, where it waits until the HUD is back), and whenever you are driving a car that is not calibrated yet (first car after starting the app, a car change), once per car, not on every unpause. *Shift at redline* then only appears once you have actually driven since the reset (new gear-1 data), so the *Calibration started* message is no longer overwritten at once. Each kind of message has its own slot: a new one replaces the one of the same kind that is still showing (the three calibration messages share one pill, Gearbox ON then OFF becomes one pill saying OFF) instead of stacking up. They fade out after about 2.5 s, up to 5 at once, always stacked vertically. Switch the whole thing and each message type on or off in Mini-Settings → Overlay → Notifications, and pick where on the screen they appear (3×3 anchor, default centre) on the Overlay tab.
- **HUD minimap settings in Mini-Settings**: the cog-wheel Mini-Settings gets an **Overlay** tab next to Dashboard with the in-game minimap's view options, like the Dashboard map's: lock north-up (the arrow turns instead of the map), north up when stopped, smooth rotation, use movement direction, compass and the two zoom radii.
- **Input permission check (Linux)**: at startup the app checks that it can read `/dev/input` (hotkeys) and write `/dev/uinput` (Gearbox / Backfire key presses). If not, a dialog shows what is missing and the exact command to fix it (with a Copy button; log out and back in afterwards). The X closes it for this session; *Don't remind me again* silences it. Setup has a new **Input Permissions** category below Window Detection with a status light per item and a *Remind me on startup* checkbox.
- **Debug tab: "Derived from telemetry"**: the Debug tab is now split in two halves. The left keeps the raw packet fields; the right shows what the app derives from them: whether the game counts as paused and which rule fired, the calibrated max RPM, the gearbox's selected / effective mode, the map season, and the car's make and model read from your own FH6 install. Display only for now.
- **Experimental pause detection**: a new checkbox in Setup → Network (on by default). The in-game HUD, and the gearbox's drift detection, now treat the garage as paused: when the car is level, completely motionless and the handbrake is fully on, the HUD hides as it does on the pause menu. Hover the checkbox for details; it may miss a garage view where the car is rotated.
- **Backfire: Limit max. duration**: a new checkbox under *RPM interval* on the Backfire tab. When on, a slider (100-5000 ms, default 1500) caps how long backfire may run continuously; after that it stops even if RPM is still in range, and starts again once you touch the throttle or downshift.
